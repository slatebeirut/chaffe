How it works

Four steps. One loop.

01

Roasteries bag their chaff. We pick it up on a fixed day, one or two trips a week, with a receipt each time.

Why coffee chaff

Light as paper. Rich as compost.

Chaff is the dry husk that flies off the bean during roasting. It is clean, sterile from the heat, and full of nitrogen. Today it goes in the roastery's trash.

  • Feeds the soilNitrogen rich, breaks down fast, feeds plants and microbes.
  • Loosens and mulchesImproves structure, holds moisture, keeps weeds down.
  • Nothing new is madeWe reuse what already exists. No synthetic inputs.
