How it's made

Four steps from roastery to soil.

01

Collect

Roasteries hand us the chaff that comes off the beans during roasting. It would otherwise be thrown away.

02

Blend

We mix 30% coffee chaff with 70% organic fertilizer.

03

Test

The prototype goes to the lab. We share what the tests show, nothing more.

04

Pack

Sealed in labelled pouches with the ingredients and how to use it.

The product

A soil amendment with one simple recipe.

Coffee chaff is the light, papery skin that separates from the bean in the roaster. We blend it with organic fertilizer so it goes back into the ground.
