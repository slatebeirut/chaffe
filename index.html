How it works

Three steps. One loop.

01

Collect

Cafés drop used pucks into our sealed bins. No sorting, no smell, no extra work.

02

Deliver

We pick up on schedule, dry and screen the grounds, then route them to nearby farms.

03

Grow

Farmers work the grounds into soil for richer, healthier harvests.

Why coffee grounds

Every puck is a dose of nutrition.

Spent grounds carry the minerals plants need most. Thrown away, they rot in landfill. Returned to the soil, they feed the next harvest.

  • Nitrogen, phosphorus, potassiumThe core nutrients of any fertilizer, naturally.
  • Holds water, loosens soilBetter structure and less irrigation.
  • Nothing new is madeWe reuse what already exists. No synthetic inputs.
Benefits

What the soil gets.

Nitrogen

Feeds earthworms and beneficial bacteria.

Pest deterrent

Naturally discourages slugs and snails.
